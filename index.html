ABOUT US

This year’s graduation showcase celebrates the design journeys behind the graduating cohort from the Division of Industrial Design, shaped by curiosity, rigor, and intent.

The works on display sit at the intersection of play and precision, imagination and purpose. This duality reflects how the designers think, make and adapt. Join us at the DID Graduation Show 2026.

LOCATE US

By Public Transport

MRT:

Take the Circle Line to Kent Ridge MRT (CC24)
Head to Exit E to Geneo, Level 1

Bus:

Take public bus no. 92/92A from Buona Vista MRT station.
Other buses available include 14, 33, 97, 166, 197, 963/963e.

By Car

From AYE, take the Normanton Park or South Buona Vista Road exit via the new slip road and then turn into Science Park Drive.

Parking:

FREE PARKING from 11:30 am to 2:00pm and 6:00 pm to 11:59 pm on weekdays, plus all day on weekends and public holidays.
